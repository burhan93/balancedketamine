What is Ketamine Infusion Therapy?

Ketamine infusion therapy involves administration of ketamine in a clinical setting. Treatment details, potential risks, benefits, and eligibility should be discussed with a healthcare professional. Uses for particular conditions may be off-label; ask your clinician about the relevant context.
